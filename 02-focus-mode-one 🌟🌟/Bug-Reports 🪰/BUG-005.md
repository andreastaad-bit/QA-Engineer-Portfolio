# BUG-005 - Doodling Video Freezes After Extending Session Duration

## Summary

Increasing the duration of the Doodling session causes the video to freeze after the original default duration, while the session timer continues running.

---

## Bug ID

**FMOA-005**

---

## Severity

**Major**

---

## Priority

**High**

---

## Environment

- **Application:** Focus Mode On
- **Website:** https://focusmodeon.lat/
- **Browser:** Opera GX
- **Operating System:** Windows 11
- **Browser Engine:** Chromium 149.0.7827.197

---

## Preconditions

- Open the Focus Mode On website.
- Navigate to the study schedule editor.

---

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the Focus Mode On website.
2. Go to the study schedule configuration.
3. Increase the **Doodling** session duration from **10 minutes** to **15 minutes**.
4. Start the study session.
5. Wait until the original 10-minute mark.

---

## Expected Result

The doodling video should continue playing normally for the entire configured duration (15 minutes).

---

## Actual Result

Once the original 10-minute duration is reached, the doodling video freezes on the last frame while the session timer continues counting down until the end of the 15-minute session.

---

## Workaround

No workaround available.

---

## Status

Unresolved
