# BUG-003 - AI Session Generator Crashes the Application

## Summary

Submitting a custom prompt to the Gemini AI Session Generator causes the application to crash, leaving the page completely blank.

---

## Bug ID

**FMOA-003**

---

## Severity

**Critical**

---

## Priority

**High**

---

## Environment

- **Application:** Focus Mode On
- **Website:** https://focusmodeon.lat/
- **Browser:** Opera GX
- **Operating System:** Windows 11
- **Browser Engine:** Chromium 149.0.7827.197

---

## Preconditions

- Open the Focus Mode On website.
- Navigate to the **Gemini AI Session Generator** section.

---

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the Focus Mode On website.
2. Scroll down to the **Gemini AI Session Generator**.
3. Enter a custom study session prompt.
4. Click **Generate AI Session**.
5. Wait for the generation process to complete.

---

## Expected Result

A personalized study session should be generated based on the user's prompt and displayed without affecting the application's functionality.

---

## Actual Result

After clicking **Generate AI Session**, the loading indicator appears briefly. A few seconds later, the entire page turns blank, making the application unusable until it is refreshed.

---

## Workaround

Refresh the page and try again.

---

## Status

Partly solved, no more crashing on the app but generator still not available
