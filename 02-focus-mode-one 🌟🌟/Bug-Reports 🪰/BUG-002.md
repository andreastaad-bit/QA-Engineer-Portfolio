# BUG-002 - Gemini Generator Templates Do Not Update Video Content

## Summary

The predefined Gemini Generator templates do not modify the generated study session. Selecting a different template does not change the video visuals or the generated audio frequencies.

---

## Bug ID

**FMOA-002**

---

## Severity

**Major**

---

## Priority

**Medium**

---

## Environment

- **Application:** Focus Mode On
- **Website:** https://focusmodeon.lat/
- **Browser:** Opera GX
- **Operating System:** Windows 11
- **Browser Engine:** Chromium 149.0.7827.197

---

## Preconditions

- Open the Focus Mode On website.
- Navigate to the **Gemini Smart Generator** section.

---

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the Focus Mode On website.
2. Scroll down to the **Gemini Smart Generator** section.
3. Select any of the three available study templates.
4. Start the generated study session.
5. Compare the generated video and audio with the default session.

---

## Expected Result

Selecting a different template should generate a different study session, including updated brainwave frequencies, video visuals, and session configuration according to the selected template.

---

## Actual Result

Selecting a different template does not modify the generated video or the audio frequencies. The only visible changes are the study session title and the timer duration.

---

## Workaround

No workaround available.
