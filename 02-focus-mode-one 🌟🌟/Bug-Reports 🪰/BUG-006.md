# BUG-006 - Doodling Audio Does Not Start Automatically

## Summary

The background audio for the Doodling session does not start automatically when the session begins. Audio playback only starts after pausing and resuming the session.

---

## Bug ID

**FMOA-006**

---

## Severity

**Major**

---

## Priority

**Medium**

---

## Environment

- **Application:** Focus Mode On
- **Website:** https://focusmodeon.lat/
- **Browser:** Opera GX
- **Operating System:** Windows 11
- **Browser Engine:** Chromium 149.0.7827.197

---

## Preconditions

- Open the Focus Mode On website.
- Ensure the Doodling session is available.

---

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the Focus Mode On website.
2. Start the **10-minute Doodling** session.
3. Observe the animation and listen for the background audio.

---

## Expected Result

The doodling animation should begin playing together with its corresponding background music and ambient sounds automatically.

---

## Actual Result

The doodling animation starts correctly, but no audio is played. The background music only starts after the user pauses the session and presses **Play** again.

---

## Workaround

Pause the session and resume playback to activate the audio.

---
